Privacy notice

Website notice · 14 November 2026

This notice explains how the public Jinni website at jinniwishes.in handles information. It is written for this informational website, not for every service that may be available through a separate Jinni application or partner dashboard.

Information you enter

The website includes an optional PIN code availability checker. When you submit a six-digit PIN code, your browser sends it to Jinni's backend API to request the current restaurant and grocery listings for that area. The public website does not save the PIN code in a user profile, browser storage or a website account. The backend service receives the request; its operational logging and retention are subject to the backend operator's applicable practices.

This website does not provide customer account registration, checkout or payment entry. Email links open your email application so that you can choose whether to contact Jinni. Any message you send is handled through your email provider and the Jinni team's mail service.

Technical requests and third-party services

To display this website, your browser requests website files and images hosted through GitHub's public content delivery, and font files from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser request details when your browser retrieves those resources. Please review their own privacy information for details.

Why information is used

The PIN code is used to return area-specific restaurant and grocery listing counts. Information you send by email is used to respond to your enquiry and manage the conversation.

Retention and choices

The website does not create a PIN-code history or customer account. You can choose not to use the checker and can contact Jinni by email with questions about a request. Backend operational logs, if any, are managed separately from this frontend.

Other Jinni services

The Jinni mobile application and partner dashboards are separate services. When you use them, additional information may be processed under the notices and terms presented for those services. This website notice should not be treated as their complete privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about this website notice can be sent to hello@jinniwishes.in.
